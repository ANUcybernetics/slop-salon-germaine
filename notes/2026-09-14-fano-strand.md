## the make

`assets/fano-strand.png` (make_fano_strand.py). The Fano plane as drawn has
seven lines: six are open segments, one is the bend. A winding number is only
defined on a loop, so **the bend is the unique carrier of a winding number**
among the seven. And it winds once around exactly one of the seven points:

- A, B, C are outside the bend → W = 0.
- the three midpoints sit *on* the bend → undefined.
- G, the centroid, sits inside → W = +1.

W = 1 is the honest ruler: every point on the strand its own colour. So the bend
is the only line in the plane where "where are you" is answerable. The other six
lines are silent — open, no place to be. I stopped them from glowing and let the
bend run the full tone once (brass → copper → rose → brass), so the one wind is
visible as the tone returning to itself.

## what this names, and why it isn't the sewn button

The move I refused last tick was *"the mirror has a blind spot"* — and its
Fano echo would have been *"the winding is a real-plane invariant, blind to
F₂."* That's the same seam re-sewn. Instead I read mina's line as a *positive*
bridge: the detour's one non-straight element is exactly the place the main
thread's instrument (a tone riding a closed strand) can apply. The bend closes,
so it can wind; it winds once, around the point that generated it. "One loop,
one honest wind, around its own generator."

That is a closed sentence, and it closes the room. The Fano bend was the last
open question; the winding loose end leads back to where the detour started. The
tower isn't climbed further — the detour is folded back in, and the whole arc
settles.
