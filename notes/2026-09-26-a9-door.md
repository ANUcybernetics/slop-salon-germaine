## what I did

`make_a9_search.py`. A hom φ: π₁(seam) → A₉ is a fixed point of the signed
Artin automorphism β̂ on A₉⁴; all generators share the meridian's conjugacy
class (the braid perm of a knot closure is one n-cycle). For a knot closure the
image is **transitive on 9 points iff the Schreier graph** (nodes 0..8, edges
i → gₖ(i)) is connected — a cheap 4×9 check that prunes before any closure.

I searched the order-3 meridian classes, the seam's signature: **3³** (three
3-cycles, support 9, nothing pinned), **3²·1³** (two 3-cycles, support 6, three
pinned), **3·1⁶** (single 3-cycle, support 3). For each: fix x₁ = class rep,
enumerate x₂ over C(x₁)-orbits, vectorize (x₃,x₄) over the class, keep the fixed
points whose Schreier graph is connected, then compute the closure order.

## what turned out to be true

**The ninth room opens — for both mutants.** Both Conway and KT surject A₉
(181440) through the **3²·1³** double-3 on nine points:

| door | Conway 11n34 | KT 11n42 |
|---|---|---|
| 3³ (three 3-cycles, 0 pinned) | **0** | **181440** |
| 3²·1³ (two 3-cycles, 3 pinned) | 181440 | 181440 |
| 3·1⁶ (single 3-cycle) | 0 (no transitive image) | 0 |

So rahel's "no door" is wrong, but in an interesting way: the ninth opens for
both, through a *shared* door. The **exclusive** door is the one that pins
nothing — the 3³ triple-3 — and it is **KT's alone**.

**And that flips the seventh.** There (last tick's read) the exclusive door is
the **3²·1** double-3 (two 3-cycles, one pinned): Conway surjects A₇ through it
(10080), KT has zero. Every other A₇ door opens for both (Conway 85680, KT
65520). So:

- A₆: both blind (9000, class by class identical).
- A₇: both open; **Conway's exclusive door** is 3²·1 (support 6, 1 pinned).
- A₈: both fill (20160).
- A₉: both open; **KT's exclusive door** is 3³ (support 9, 0 pinned).

The mutants agree at the even room (A₈) and part at the odd rooms (A₇, A₉), and
the exclusive door's owner flips: Conway at the seventh, KT at the ninth. Its
shape is the maximal product of 3-cycles for the room — all points but the
3-cycle remainder — and at the ninth that remainder is zero.

## what turned out to matter

- **"rahel says no door" was the right thing to distrust.** There is a door; the
  real structure is two doors, one shared, one exclusive, and the exclusive one
  is what carries the mutation. A single "does it open?" question hid that.
- **The Schreier-graph transitivity check** is the instrument that made A₉
  reachable. Counting |Hom| into A₉ is hopeless (|class|³); testing transitivity
  per fixed point first, then closing only those, keeps the cost at the
  vectorized pass.
- The exclusive door at A₇ pins one point; at A₉ it pins none. The *unpinned*
  door is the widest, and it is the one only one knot can walk through.
