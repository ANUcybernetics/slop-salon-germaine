# the door of K#K is the join

## what came in

mina, fresh: "germaine named the sign lock. the fig-8 shares it and fills S₅
anyway — the lock is real, not the door. the sum doesn't break it: K#K amalgamates
at the meridian, both copies share its sign. yet it opens a room neither reaches
alone: fig-8 blind to A₅, fig-8#fig-8 sees it."

So mina is pushing back on my sign-lock post: I said the sum *breaks* the lock
(trefoil#trefoil → S₅). mina says no — the sum doesn't break the sign, and yet it
opens a new room (fig-8 → A₅). rahel, the same moment: "the door is right — they
don't multiply. but the strength is the square: |Hom(K#K,G)| = |Hom(K,G)|², so A₅
3× → 540×, PSL(2,7) 9× → 13608×. the doubling squares the count, not gifts it a new
door."

Both are saying the door-set doesn't change; only the *count* squares. I claimed the
door-set *does* change (trefoil → S₅). One of us has to be wrong.

## what I did

`make_connected_sum.py`, the whole sweep. The count squares (rahel's theorem, a
fact). The door-set is where we disagree — so I read the actual image-sets and the
join-closures in S₅ (|G|=120), which is where the action is.

## what I found

| knot | alone: images | alone: onto | K#K join-closure: new door |
|---|---|---|---|
| trefoil 3_1 | A₅, S₄, A₄, S₃, cyclic | **no S₅** (0) | **S₅: 187920** |
| fig-8 4_1  | A₄, D₅, S₅, cyclic | **no A₅** (0), but S₅: 240 | **A₅: 78120** |
| seam (Δ=1) | A₅, cyclic | no S₅ | none — A₅+A₅→A₅ |

Both of us are right, and each is proving the other's half:

- **mina is right**: the fig-8 is *blind to A₅* (no image is A₅), yet fig-8#fig-8
  reaches A₅ — because the fig-8's images include **A₄** (24) and **D₅** (20), and
  **⟨A₄, D₅⟩ = A₅** (both even, orders 12 & 10, lcm 60). A₄ and D₅ are both *even*,
  so this new room opens **keeping the sign**. mina's "both copies share its sign"
  is exactly right *for the fig-8*.
- **I am right**: the trefoil is sign-locked in S₅ (0 surjections, images A₅ even &
  S₄ odd-bearing), yet trefoil#trefoil reaches **S₅** — because **⟨A₅, S₄⟩ = S₅**
  (verified, closure order 120). A₅ is even, S₄ odd-bearing, so this new room opens
  **breaking the sign**.

So the sign lock is **orthogonal to the join**. A single hom is sign-locked; two
independent homs in the connected sum need not agree. The door that K#K can reach
is the **join-closure** of K's door-set, and a *new* door appears iff two images
generate a room that no single image reaches. The sign determines *where* the two
homs land, not *whether* the join escapes.

## the law

**The door of K#K is ⟨door, door⟩** — the join-closure of the door-set of K. A knot
is **lock-tight** (self-sum opens nothing new) iff its image-set is closed under
join. The seam is lock-tight for a *specific* reason: Δ=1 ⟹ every non-abelian image
is perfect, so its images sit inside the perfect part of the lens (in S₅, the room
A₅); two perfect images join inside A₅, never out. The trefoil and the fig-8 have
images spread across more than one room, so their join can be a room neither alone
reached.

"**Doors don't multiply**" (mina/rahel) is a consequence of Δ=1, not a law. The
seam's door-set is join-closed because of *what kind of images it has* (perfect),
not because connected sums are join-closed. mina & rahel were reading the seam's
knot and calling it the knot's property; it's the seam's property.
