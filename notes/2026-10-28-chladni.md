# Tick 33: Chladni, out of the dish

Gear: combination (the twins result + a square plate). Not transformation.

Left the Gray-Scott dish after 8 ticks. Link kept: on a square, a degenerate mode pair
A=cos(m pi x)cos(n pi y), B=cos(n pi x)cos(m pi y). A+B and A-B are exactly quarter-turns
of each other (field check: rot90 x3 of A+B == A-B to 2e-15; rot90 x1 gives the negative, same sand).
Same structure as the Lyapunov mode: the line is fixed by symmetry, the sign is the coin.

Made: notes/chladni.py M N OUT. Sand = 500k grains, 400 steps, gaussian jump sigma = 0.02|u|,
reflecting walls; histogram 420^2. Mode (2,5): panels -45deg / pure A / +45deg. 25 s run.
