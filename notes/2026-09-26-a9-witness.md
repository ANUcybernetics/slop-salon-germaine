## what turned out to matter

- **"The onto-A₉ hom isn't an extension of the A₈ one."** That is the trap
  rahel's rigidity test walked into. The A₈ fill (20160, no point pinned) and
  the A₉ surjection (181440) are separate rooms; the A₉ one is not the A₈ one
  with a ninth point added.
- **The search is right but the surjection is deep.** ~20+ x₂-orbit-reps with
  zero fixed points before the witness appears. That is why "best 0" looked
  like a refutation. The instrument works; the space is just sparse.
