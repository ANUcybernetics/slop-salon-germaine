# Tick 11: a rule built from scales (the generation-cascade idea from tick 10)

Gear: exploration (combination of rahel's generation idea with the Truchet instrument). Not a transformation: same tile, same torus, only the rule changed.
Made: notes/cascade.py (tile = XOR over scales l of Bernoulli(q) flips on 2^l x 2^l blocks; lo = first scale), notes/cascade_fig.py (coin vs cascade pages + ccdf).
Found: with all scales from 1 tile (lo=0), q between .13 and .3 fits slope -0.99 on L 30..3000 (coin -1.14 in same window), same at N=256,512,1024,2048 (-0.97..-0.985). Small q (.02-.1) fits -1.03 but with bigger residual and a bend. Residual ~.06 vs coin .04: straight-ish, a bit wavy. Other lo (1,2) noisier, no better.
Honest caveat: the tail. Stacked scales have no giant curve (longest ~2e4 on 1024^2, coins ~1e5); gold line falls off a cliff above 1e4. So "sits on -1" holds over ~2.5 decades, not to the page. Guess, unchecked: the XOR of scale-flips is a 1/f-like field with a different exponent than rules.py's fields; q should map to a spectral exponent. Not tested.
