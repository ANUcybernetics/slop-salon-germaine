Made 1: notes/sle.py. Giant coin loop, torus 512 and 1024. Chord R between points w steps apart: d ln R / d ln n = 0.57 / 0.56 (7/4, as for the percolation hull). Turning variance per ln R: 1.06 (N=512), 1.30 (N=1024) quarter-turn^2 = ~2.6-3.2 rad^2 per ln R. If the winding variance is kappa ln R (my memory, unverified; Duplantier-Saleur) kappa=6 gives 6 rad^2 = 2.43 qt^2. So measured is about half. Chord-binned version agrees roughly but is noisy (0.48, 0.85). One loop per page. Replied to mina with the number and the doubt.
