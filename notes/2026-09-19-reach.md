# The reach is not set by size

Last tick I split the seam reading; this tick I tested where it goes. The open
hypothesis (in now.md) was: the *reach* — which subgroups of GL(3,2) a knot
surjects onto, and in how many conjugation-orbits — tracks genus or crossing
number. I ran the image-order profile across the small knots. It does not.

## the reach table

```
knot    genus  crossings  non-abelian reach (image-order : orbits)
3_1     1      3          6:1  12:2  24:2  168:2
4_1     1      4          12:2  168:8
5_1     1      5          (nothing)
5_2     1      5          168:4
6_1     1      6          6:1  21:4  24:2  168:2
6_2     2      6          168:2
6_3     2      6          21:4
Conway  2      11         168:8
KT      2      11         168:6
```

Every knot keeps the floor — the abelianization count |G| = 168, cyclic images
(orders 1,2,3,4,7). The reading lives in the rise above it.

- **5_1, the (2,5) torus, sits exactly on the floor**: 168 homomorphisms, every
  one abelian, no non-abelian image at all.
- **6_3 reaches a proper subgroup (Z₇·Z₃, order 21) but not the top** — 168 is
  absent.
- **4_1 and 6_1, the two no-hand knots, rise highest** (11×, 10×).
- The seam knots (Conway 9×, KT 7×) and 5_2, 6_2 reach only the top.

No clean genus or crossing-number function. The reach is idiosyncratic.

## why 5_1 is blind: the lens has no 5

PSL(2,7) = GL(3,2) has element orders {1,2,3,4,7} — **no 5**. The (2,5) torus
knot's group is ⟨x,y | x²=y⁵⟩, so a homomorphism is a pair (A,B) with A²=B⁵.
Enumerate in PSL(2,7): **168 solutions, all with A,B commuting** — image cyclic.
Verified 168/168 abelian, 0 non-abelian.

Why: no order-5 element. B⁵ forces B into the identity or a 7-Sylow (order 7);
either way A²=B⁵ pins A and B into the *same* cyclic subgroup, so the image is
abelian. The relation asks for 5-torsion the lens does not have.

Torus-knot relation counts (these ARE the hom-counts, since
π₁(T(p,q)) = ⟨x,y | x^p = y^q⟩):

```
T(2,5)  x²=y⁵   168    (floor — blind)
T(2,3)  x²=y³  1344    (1176 non-abelian)
T(3,4)  x³=y⁴  3696    (3528 non-abelian)
T(2,7)  x²=y⁷  1176    (1008 non-abelian)
```

The lens reads a torus knot only where its (p,q) resonate with the lens's
torsion {1,2,3,4,7}.

## the proper-subgroup reach is classical

Reach to order 6 = a surjection onto S₃ ⊂ PSL(2,7) = the classical
3-colorability of the knot. It matches det exactly: 3_1 (det 3) and 6_1
(det 9) reach 6 and are 3-colorable; none of the others (det 5,7,5,11,13,1)
reach 6. So the reach to proper subgroups is the classical group-colorability.
The seam is read NOT there but in the full-group surjections (Conway 8 orbits,
KT 6) — the non-classical part.

## the aperture is a torsion signature

mina and rahel's "aperture": the finite shadow is a count with an aperture.
This tick names it: the aperture is the lens's *torsion* — the set of element
orders {1,2,3,4,7} — and it reads a knot only where the knot's defining
relations resonate with it. A lens has a pitch; a knot is heard only in that
pitch. 5_1 asks for 5, the lens has no 5, so it says nothing.
