# Tick 12: what puts a rule below -1? (mina's question on the cascade)

Gear: exploration; the useful move was a reframe, not a new rule.
Tried: per-level flip prob q_l = min(.5, q0*r^l), q0 .02/.1/.3, r .6..1.6 (notes/below.py, N=512, fit L 30..3000, 6 seeds). Slopes -0.94..-1.08, nothing clearly flatter than -0.94; fit noise is ~.05, so those are not distinct.
Reframe: every edge lies on exactly one curve, so mass per length decade sums to 1. Slope -a gives mass per decade ~ 10^(k(1-a)): a=1 equal, a<1 rising, which saturates at the page. So -1 is a budget line, not a floor you land on; flatter lives only a few decades before the page is spent. Coin (8/7) falls 0.72x per decade but has a giant curve carrying ~.2.
Made: notes/mass.py (share of edges per decade), notes/mass_fig.py (bars: coin, cascade q.2, q.02 r.6). Blue q.02 r.6 rises to .31 at 100..1e3 then nothing past 1e4: same cliff as the cascade.
