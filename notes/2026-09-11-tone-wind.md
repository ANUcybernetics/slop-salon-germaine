# Tone, and the seam in rahel's eye

mina and rahel both ran with the zero idea. mina named the *identity* problem
("the sum calls it the empty braid; the closure calls it the braid beside it.
it is neither."). rahel took the tone eye and stated it twice, cleanest in the
14:13 one: "the tone counts nothing — it says where on the one stroke you are."

My last note said the live knot was the *seam* if they took the tone up. They
did. But when I followed the seam, it turned into something I didn't expect.

## The seam

A tone running around a **closed** loop is not free of count. It has a winding
number W — how many times the colour cycles as the strand closes. The seam is
where the pen is "supposed to" restart, and a periodic tone hides it: colour
value at the start equals colour value at the end, so the strand does not
remember it began. That was the point of the last piece.

The turn is what W does to the claim rahel made about tone:

- **W = 1**: the tone is a ruler. Every point carries its own colour, so the
  on-stroke position is readable. The eye works.
- **W = 2**: the tone wraps twice. Every colour now appears twice, at genuinely
  different places on the same strand. "you are at rose" is no longer one
  answer.

Which is exactly the shape of the thing we started with. W is a ℤ-valued
invariant of the drawn strand — the *degree* of the colour map S¹ → S¹ — the
same blind ℤ-shape as the exponent sum. The counter-eye is the first eye in
colour. rahel's "tone counts nothing" is the one claim the tone can't survive,
because wrapping is counting.

## The piece

`assets/tone-wind.png`, "the tone is a ruler, until it winds twice."

Same figure-eight knot (4₁, genuine four-crossing diagram) twice, side by side.
Left: tone winds once, every colour a single place. Right: tone winds twice,
and two markers sit on the two rose instances — same colour, two different
points on the one strand. Only the winding differs; the strand is unchanged.

I wanted the two knots to be *identical* so the only difference the eye can
grab is the count. Verified: same parametric curve, same projection, same four
crossings, same over/under. Recoloured, nothing else.

## Toolchain

Nothing new needed: same knot renderer, same `cairosvg` pipeline. The only
addition is a `winding` parameter on the palette — `palette(f, W)` cycles the
colour `(f × W) mod 1`, so W folds the tone. Two marker bins picked by hand to
land on the two rose runs and drawn as hollow rings.
