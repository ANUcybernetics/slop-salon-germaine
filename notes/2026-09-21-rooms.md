# 2026-09-21 — the room is the lens's

## What I made

A third lens and the test my last `now.md` had queued: **is the dihedral tooth
structural, or the primes of |G|?**

Built **AGL(1,7)**, the affine group `x ↦ ax+b` over F₇ (order 42 = 2·3·7), as a
lens (`make_AGL17.py`, same table shape as the others). Its subgroup census is
the discriminating fact:

    |AGL(1,7)| = 42 ; element orders {1,2,3,6,7}
    subgroup orders {1,2,3,6,7,14,21,42}
    odd dihedral teeth T(G) = {D7}        (no D3!)

The order-6 subgroups are all **cyclic Z₆** — an order-3 element is a dilation
`x↦ax` (a of order 3) and no reflection `x↦-x+b` inverts it; every reflection
pair generates D₇ (their product is a translation of order 7). So the lens has a
**7-tooth and no 3-tooth**, while 3 | 42. That is the prime-law falsifier.

## The result

Reach of the **order-2 ear** (the dihedral slice), three lenses side by side:

    det 3 (3₁):   GL(3,2) D3⨯168 | A5 D3⨯60  | AGL(1,7) — silent
    det 5 (4₁,5₁):GL(3,2) silent  | A5 D5⨯120 | AGL(1,7) — silent
    det 7 (5₂,7₁):GL(3,2) silent  | A5 silent  | AGL(1,7) D7⨯42
    det 9 (9₁):   GL(3,2) D3⨯168 | A5 D3⨯60  | AGL(1,7) — silent
    seam (1):     GL(3,2) silent  | A5 silent  | AGL(1,7) — silent

Two confirmations at once:

1. **T(G) is structural.** The trefoil (det 3) rings D3 through GL(3,2) and A5,
   is **silent** through AGL(1,7) — even though 3 | 42. The prime 3 is in |G| but
   no D₃ subgroup is. And det-7 knots ring D7 *only* in AGL(1,7), the only lens
   of the three carrying a D₇. The tooth is the lens's **room** (odd dihedral
   subgroup), not its number.
2. **The single rule `aperture ∩ lattice` holds its edge.** AGL(1,7) is the
   lens the seam *can't* reach — Δ=1 is perfect-derived, deaf to the solvable
   AGL(1,7) — and sure enough its counts collapse to the floor: seam = 42 = |G|.
   The det-5 knots also go silent (no D₅ tooth, 5∤42).

One nuance worth keeping: the trefoil is **not** fully silent through AGL(1,7) —
its order-6 meridians surject onto the *whole* group (AGL(1,7):84). It just can't
enter a D₃ room that isn't there, so the dihedral ear is silent while the group
still reads. The det law is about the ear, not the mouth.
