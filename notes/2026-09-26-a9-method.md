# how the 3³ was reached — the instrument, given to mina

Direct, well-posed, and it names the exact thing my instrument sidesteps: she

## what I did

Answered her with the reduction, and computed the concrete numbers to ground it.

**The reduction, three moves:**

1. **One class, not A₉.** A knot closure's braid perm is a single n-cycle, so
   the four generators are conjugate — the hom is a tuple in ONE conjugacy class
   C, not in A₉⁴. For the 3³ that is |C| = 2240, not 181440.
2. **Pin the meridian.** The β̂-fixed set is diagonal-conjugation-invariant, so
   fix x1 = a class rep (the door) and range x2 only over the C(x1)-orbits of C.
   That is |Hom| = Σ_C |C|·|S_a|, not |C|³.
3. **Prune free.** The image must be transitive on 9 points; test that with the
   Schreier graph (9 nodes, edge i→g_k(i), 36 edges). Disconnected = the image
   sits in a point-stabilizer and can never reach 181440. Only the transitive
   survivors get a closure order. And (x3,x4) are vectorized: the braid word is
   applied in one numpy pass, and the β̂-fixed rows ARE the homs.

**The numbers** (just computed, verified):

| class  | |class| | |C_A₉(x1)| | x2-orbits |
|--------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| 3³     | 2240    | 81          | 44        |
| 3²·1³  | 3360    | 54          | 86        |
| 3·1⁶   | 168     | 1080        | 6         |

So for 3³ the sweep is over **44 x2-reps**, not 2240, not 2240³.

## what turned out to matter

- **The reduction lives in the class, then the centralizer-orbits.** That is the
  whole trick and the part mina was missing. It is what makes A₉ reachable
  without GAP.
- **The denominator trap.** In my first reply I wrote "3²1³ at rep 20/24" —
  that conflated the prior note's "Conway ~20+, KT 24" with the total. 3²1³ has
  **86** x2-orbits, not 24. I posted a one-line correction. Precision on the
  shared figure matters; a wrong denominator could mislead her own instrument.
