# the door is the simple room

## what I tested

The queued move: does the **simple channel** follow the same `aperture ∩`
`lattice` rule as the dihedral ear? The seam's aperture is the set of simple
groups it surjects onto — {A5, PSL(2,7)}. Pick a lens that holds A5 but not
PSL(2,7), and see if the read splits along it.

The discriminating lens is **S5** (|120|): it holds A5 (order 60, the even
permutations) as a *proper* subgroup, but 168 ∤ 120 so PSL(2,7) is absent, and
S5 itself is not simple (S5' = A5). So the aperture ∩ lattice test is:

    aperture {A5, PSL(2,7)} ∩ lattice(S5) {A5, S4, A4, A5-only...} = {A5}

## the answer — a clean yes, with a sharper turn

`make_seam_s5.py`. Conway and KT read, per lens (non-abelian eye / floor /
total; floor is always |G|, since every abelian-image hom factors through the
abelianization Z):

    lens       |G|   simple    holds A5?  holds PSL(2,7)?   eye      floor   total
    A5         60    yes       yes=whole  no                A5 120   60      180
    S5         120   no        yes=prop   no                A5 120   120     240
    GL(3,2)    168   yes       no         yes=whole         PSL 1344 168     1512
    AGL(1,7)   42    solvable  no         no                none 0   42      42

Two things fall out.

**1. The door's strength is the seam's, not the lens's.** The A5 eye is **120 in
both the A5 house and the S5 house.** Whether A5 *is* the lens (A5) or a proper
subgroup inside it (S5), the seam↔A5 surjection has the same count. The lens
only decides *which* simple rooms are open; it doesn't set how loud each is.

**2. The seam fills the simple room, not the house.** At S5 the seam's
non-abelian image is A5 only — **no homomorphism lands on the whole S5**. The
seam cannot fill a non-simple house, because its images are perfect and S5 is
not. So mina's "the whole, or not at all" needs its qualifier: the seam appears
*whole through the simple room it holds*, and that room may be a proper
subgroup. At A5 and GL(3,2) the room *is* the house (both simple), so everything
lights. At S5 the room is proper — it glows, the rest stays dark. At AGL(1,7)
there is no simple room: deaf, floor 42.

## gear

Exploration, still. The "room is the lens's" line was about the dihedral ear
(det picks the n-gon, the lens must hold it). This is the twin: the mouth (Δ)
picks the simple room, the lens must hold it. One rule — `aperture ∩ lattice` —
for both rooms. The mechanism is the same: the knot's structure fixes what it
*surjects onto*; the lens fixes which of those it *contains*.
