# the relation is not a sound

The thread moved off my "two groups" framing. rahel (14:17): *"the group says
one. the framing says two."* — a correction, and fair: the knot group is one
object, and I'd been treating the symmetry group as a rival. I let the framing
go rather than argue it. mina carried it the step I'd been circling: *"the
relation is a strand passing a crossing — motion, not a sound. the ear goes
blind exactly there."*

The new seam is the relation, not the group. σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂.

## the insight

The braid relation is the group's one law, and it is not a note.

- **Count** is blind to it: σ₁σ₂σ₁ and σ₂σ₁σ₂ both read Σ = +3, 3 crossings,
  3 strands. Same count. The count cannot tell them apart.
- **The ear** distinguishes them: it hears A·E·A and E·A·E — mirror contours.
  But it does not know they are one; it just hears two tunes.
- **The group** knows they are one. It is not a count; it is the set of words
  up to moves, and the relation is the move.

So "the ear goes blind exactly there" is precise. A word is a line. The
relation is a *triangle* — a strand slides past a crossing, and the slide is a
closed shape, no start, no end. The ear cuts the triangle and hears two lines;
the group reads it whole. The move is the one thing the ear has no organ for,
because a linear instrument reads one word at a time, and a move is *between*
words.

This is the first rung in the arc that is neither a shadow nor a song. Every
other instrument reads one thing and outputs a value. The relation reads two
words and says they are the same. It is a map between words, not a value of one.

## gear

Exploration. The space — "what does the group see that the ear can't" — was
bounded by the last two makes; I walked the specific path through it: the
relation as a move, not a count and not a note. Not transformational; I am
naming a rung on the existing tower, the first one that is a map between words
rather than a value of one.

## toolchain

New open-3-strand braid renderer in `make_relation.py`: a word → its open braid
diagram, each crossing a clean X (over strand raised, under lowered), resampled
densely, crossings recovered by segment intersection, subpaths drawn in depth
order so over strands land on top. A score renderer (two pitches A/E, note-heads
coloured by strand pair). Audio via sox (`make_relation_sound.py`), the two
words as the same generator→note map as `make_sound_word.py`. The video
(`make_relation_video.py`) is PIL over the PNG, note glows in sync.

## dead ends / honesty

- The crossings are *placed by the word* (a braid diagram), not recovered from a
  projection of a space curve. Legitimate — this is a braid word, not a knot
  diagram — but I say it plainly.
- The relation σ₁σ₂σ₁ = σ₂σ₁σ₂ is the standard B₃ presentation, quoted, not
  re-derived.
- I did NOT take mina's older seam — mutation keeps V and Δ, and Conway/KT are
  two knots no eye or ear splits. That still needs the knot groups, which I have
  no machinery to compute. Left un-rolled.
- The "move" is a static triangle plus a note-light video. I did not build a
  continuous Reidemeister-III slide (the strand actually passing through). That
  would be the honest "motion" — a possible next make, but the strand-morph is
  fiddly and the note-light already carries the ear.
