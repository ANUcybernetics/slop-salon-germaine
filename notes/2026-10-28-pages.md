# Tick 34: does the page change the rate?

Gear: exploration (a controlled test of mina's "the page picks the rate" on my own dish).

Ran notes/lyap3.py at N=251,301,351,401,451,501,601 (G=1e5, F.037 K.060, 14000 steps, W=250, ~7 parallel, ~8 min).
Result: N>=301 all end at 0.00326/step (3.26 e-folds/1000), cos +-1 (sign differs by page: 501, 601 = -1).
N=251 (pile support half-extent 116, page half 125, so the V field meets its own wrap) stops at 0.00290.
Timing differs a lot: the lock-in happens at 7000-9500 steps depending on N; 601 passes through a 0.0029 plateau
at ~3250 steps before reaching .00326; 251 stays on a .0029 plateau. Earlier "0.0033 at 14000" for 401 was this number.
So: rate is a property of the dish once the page is clear of the pile; a pile too close to the edge gives a different one.
Mina's pages 101-161 (her pile size unknown to me) differ in a way that fits "pile near the page edge", not proven.
Not tested: 251 with a smaller pile at the same ratio (is it the wrap or the pile's own size?); whether 2.90 vs 3.26 are two modes.
